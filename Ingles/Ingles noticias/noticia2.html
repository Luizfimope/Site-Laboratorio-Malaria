Pedro Henrique Oliveira Pereira, during the production of the video pitch for the competition.

Pedro Henrique Oliveira Pereira, a member of GEIM, was awarded first place in the "I Do IC, and You?" contest.

Student Pedro Henrique Oliveira Pereira won first place in the "I Do Scientific Initiation, and You?" contest, an initiative aimed at valuing and publicizing the scientific production developed by undergraduate research students.

With dedication and commitment to his project, Pedro Henrique stood out among the participants for the relevance and quality of his research, which caught the attention of the evaluation committee for its originality and potential impact. The achievement reaffirms the importance of encouraging academic research in the university environment and serves as inspiration for other students who wish to pursue a career in science.

The "I Do Scientific Inquiry, and You?" contest aims to recognize young researchers and bring the academic community closer to scientific research activities, promoting the exchange of experiences and the appreciation of innovative projects.
